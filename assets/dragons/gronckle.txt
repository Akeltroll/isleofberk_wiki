== Trivia ==

* Feeding Gronckles granite, diorite, deepslate, or stone will have them produce raw [[Gronckle Iron]] which can be smelted in a furnace or crafted into [[Gronckle Iron|Gronckle iron ingots]]
* Naming any Gronckle "Meatlug" with a nametag will cause it to change into the Meatlug (10) variant
* Gronckle eggs explode and create fire when they hatch in a 3 block radius around them.
