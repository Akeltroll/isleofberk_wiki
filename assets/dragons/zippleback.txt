== Trivia ==

* Naming any Hideous Zippleback "Barf and Belch" with a nametag causes it to change into the Pistill (11) variant
* Most of the skins are references to different characters from the games, with variants 5, 7 being references to dragons in Rise of Berk, and variant 2 being a reference to the character from School of Dragons
* Variant 6 is a reference to the dragon owned by Bucket and Mulch in the How to Train you Dragon TV series
* Variant 10 "Kandy 'n' Kane" is unobtainable outside of the /summon command.
