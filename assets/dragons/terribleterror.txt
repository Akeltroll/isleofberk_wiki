== Trivia ==

* These dragons have the ability to sit on the user's head and shoulders, with up to 3 at a time. Make sure the dragon is in follow mode before right clicking it.
* They will pick up meat drops off the ground
* They will hold any food item if given to them directly, they will also eat any food item they hold after awhile, and subsequently gain any affects from it.
* Variant 8 "Dragonfruit" is only obtainable through the use of the /summon command.
/summon isleofberk:terrible_terror ~ ~ ~ {variant:#}
