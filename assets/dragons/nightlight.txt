== Trivia ==
*A Night Light can only be spawned by breeding a [[Light fury|Light Fury]] and a [[Night fury]]
*Due to Night Lights not spawning naturally, they are not listed in the config They are also the only dragon that is not listed at all on the WIX page
*There are 11 variants of the Nightlight  
*Variant 8 is the only dragon with Heterochromia, a condition that causes each eye to be different colours
*When breeding two Night Lights together, you'll get a Night Light egg. However, when breeding a Night Light with a Night Fury or Light Fury, you will get a complete Night Fury or Light Fury egg.
