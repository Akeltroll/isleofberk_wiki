== Trivia ==

* It's ability will burn the rider once they dismount, it's recommended to submerge the dragon in water before dismounting (this toggles off the ability)
* It is unable to use it's ability in the rain
* Naming any Monstrous Nightmare "Hookfang" with a nametag causes it to change into the Hookfang (10) variant
* There is a rare Melanistic variant (9) that has a 1/20 spawnrate in the wild. Eggs have a significantly lower spawnrate for this variant.
* Variant 5 "Wolfsbane" is probably a reference to the Rise of Berk mobile game, it is the first monstrous nightmare obtained in the mobile app.
* There are now 14 variants in total.
