=== Trivia ===

* Wild speed stingers will attack all "livestock animals" (cows, sheep, chicken, pig) whereas tamed speed stingers will exclusively attack pigs
* All speed stingers can walk on water
* Due to the fact that all speedstingers generate with their nest structure, they are not in the spawnweights config.
* They can jump up to 2 blocks high
* Speedstingers regenerate in their structures over time.
* It is not uncommon to see variant 4 speedstingers in the same nests as the other variants
* Speedstingers can't be ridden
* you can find up to 12 eggs in a den
